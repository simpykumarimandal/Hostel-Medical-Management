Frequently Asked Questions

How do I file a claim?
You can file a claim through the "New Claim Request" button. You'll need to upload bills and prescriptions.
What is cashless treatment?
Cashless treatment allows you to get medical care at network hospitals without paying upfront.
When does my policy renew?
Your policy renews annually on December 31st. You'll receive reminders 45 days before expiry.
Are pre-existing conditions covered?
Pre-existing conditions are covered after 30 days of continuous coverage under the policy.
