Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to resolve a complaint?

Resolution times vary based on complaint priority. High priority complaints are typically resolved within 3 days, medium within 7 days, and low priority within 14 days.

Can I update my complaint after submission?

Yes, you can add additional information to your complaint by using the "Update Complaint" feature in the complaint tracking section.

What should I do if my complaint hasn't been resolved in the expected time?

If your complaint exceeds the expected resolution time, you can use the escalation feature to bring it to the attention of higher authorities.

Quick Solutions

Wi-Fi Connectivity Issues

Try restarting your router, checking cable connections, or contacting the IT department directly at it-support@vitbhopal.ac.in

Library Book Availability

Check the online library catalog first. If the book is listed as available but you can't find it, ask the librarian for assistance.
